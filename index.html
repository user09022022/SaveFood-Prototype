Scope & limitations

The prototype copies the activities, labels, colors, general layout organization, and demo flows inspected in the repository. It is not a pixel-verified emulator capture. It does not share the Android database or runtime. Manager orders and customer orders intentionally use separate demo datasets, as in the inspected Java source.
